PAGE 2 — THE EXPERIENCE
Down the Rabbit Hole is a dynamic after-hours experience at AWKN Ranch designed around
music, wellness, hospitality, connection, performance, recovery, and discovery. Guests flow
throughout the experience rather than gathering in a single room, which allows the night to
unfold through multiple environments, each with its own purpose and energy.

The experience includes the Maloka Dome, AWKN Wellness Lounge, Temple activations, Zen
Den and The Fire Pit & Zen Den, Elixir Bar, Connection Garden, Hammock Garden, Stargazing
Lounge, private house hospitality, and a Sunrise Reset. Together, these spaces create a night
that feels expansive without losing the beauty of real connection.

For our partners, that creates something far more valuable than a standard event placement:
multiple ways to become part of how the night actually flows, feels, and is remembered.
PAGE 3 — WHY THIS NIGHT
MATTERS
The value of Down the Rabbit Hole is not simply attendance. It is the combination of the people,
the setting, and the amount of time they spend inside the experience.

The guest community includes founders, speakers, investors, clinicians, wellness leaders,
creators, practitioners, researchers, entrepreneurs, and people actively shaping emerging
conversations around wellness, longevity, psychedelic science, human performance,
consciousness, culture, and technology.

Down the Rabbit Hole gives that community an environment built for more than scheduled
programming. Guests are moving, eating, drinking, recovering, dancing, participating in
activations, discovering new relationships, brands, and partners.

For the right partner, that creates a rare opportunity to move from visibility into real interaction.
PAGE 4 — THE PROPERTY
BECOMES THE EXPERIENCE
The ranch allows the event to function as a collection of distinct environments that feel like
jumping thru portals rather than one large party.

The Maloka Dome carries the music and late-night energy. The AWKN Wellness Lounge gives
guests access to IV hydration, NAD+, recovery, and longevity experiences. The Temple hosts
rotating activations such as sound healing, breathwork, nervous-system work, movement, and
guided experiences.

The Zen Den and fire pit create a slower space for connection.

The Elixir Bar becomes a destination for functional beverages, hydration and botanical blends.

The Connection Garden and Hammock Garden offer softer spaces for conversation, readings
and rest.

The Stargazing Lounge gives guests another place to settle into the night,

while the House creates a private hospitality experience for select guests and VIPs. The night
closes with Sunrise Reset.

Each environment gives our partners a different role to play without forcing the same
sponsorship model across the entire event.




PAGE 5 — THE PARTNERSHIP
PHILOSOPHY
A strong brand integration should feel useful, relevant and natural to the experience.
A hydration partner should be where people need hydration. A recovery company should be
inside recovery. A beverage brand should be poured and tasted. A furniture or design partner
should shape how a space feels. A technology partner should be placed where guests can
actually interact with the product. A hospitality brand should live inside the guest experience, not
simply appear beside it.

The goal is not to place as many logos as possible throughout the property. The goal is to create
partnerships guests can experience.

That is where the value increases.




PAGE 6 — CHOOSE YOUR PORTAL
Brands can enter Down the Rabbit Hole in several ways depending on what they provide and
what they want to accomplish.

A partner may own a major environment, power a specific experience, support wellness or
recovery, create a food or beverage moment, integrate into VIP hospitality, shape the arrival,
provide furniture or design, support production and security, contribute media or PR, provide
marketing materials, or build a custom activation specifically for the event.

Signature opportunities may include Maloka Dome presented by a lead partner, AWKN
Wellness Lounge powered by a wellness brand, Official Recovery Partner, Official Hydration
Partner, Official Elixir Partner, Official Hospitality Partner, Official VIP Gifting Partner, Official
Arrival Partner, Official Transportation Partner, Official Media Partner, Official Lighting Partner
and Sunrise Reset presented by a lead partner.

Select categories may be exclusive.

PAGE 9 — THE HOUSE + VIP
EXPERIENCE
The House creates a more private hospitality layer for select invited guests, talent, speakers,
founders and VIPs staying on property.

This is where brand integration becomes more personal. Products can be placed in rooms
before arrival, incorporated into bathroom amenities, included in bedside recovery, stocked in
refrigerators, integrated into morning wellness or presented through curated gifting.

The value of this environment is not mass distribution. It is thoughtful placement within a
premium guest experience.

For certain brands, one well-placed product in the right room can carry more value than
hundreds of samples handed out without context.




PAGE 10 — CURATED VIP GIFTING
VIP gifting will be curated around usefulness, quality and relevance to the experience.

Products may be included in VIP bags, placed directly inside guest rooms, incorporated into
recovery kits, used as welcome gifts, included in bathroom amenities, offered during morning
hospitality or presented as departure gifts.
The purpose is not to fill bags. The purpose is to put strong products into the hands of guests
who are likely to use them, remember them and potentially continue the relationship with the
brand after the event.

VIP gifting partners may also receive additional visibility depending on contribution level and
placement.




PAGE 11 — ARRIVAL + GUEST
EXPERIENCE
The guest experience begins before they reach the party.

Guests will arrive through a dedicated drop-off area with a roundabout, illuminated AWKN
letters, a check-in point, security and a pathway leading into the event. Select guests and
partners may receive VIP parking and priority arrival access.

This creates natural sponsorship opportunities around transportation, arrival, check-in, guest
welcome, signage, photography and hospitality.

For a lead arrival partner, this can become one of the most valuable positions of the night
because every guest passes through it.




PAGE 12 — WHERE WE ARE
SEEKING PARTNERS
We are opening partnerships across the parts of the event that directly shape guest experience
and production.
Those areas include experience and activations, wellness and recovery, food and beverage, VIP
and house hospitality, environment and design, furniture and decor, arrival and transportation,
lighting and production, security, media and PR, photography and video, marketing materials,
guest amenities, event infrastructure and custom activations.

This allows brands to participate through monetary sponsorship, in-kind contribution or a
combination of both without forcing every partner into the same structure.




PAGE 13 — MONETARY
PARTNERSHIPS
Monetary partners receive the deepest level of ownership, visibility and access within Down the
Rabbit Hole.

Depending on investment level, benefits may include premium placement on the event website
and registration page, approved recognition through Beyond Wonderland, naming rights,
category exclusivity, premium onsite branding, dedicated activation ownership, product
integration, VIP credentials, guest tickets, VIP parking, priority arrival, select hospitality access,
social recognition, press inclusion, professional photography, video content, custom brand
content and post-event recognition.

The larger the investment, the deeper the partner can be integrated into the event. Higher
investment should unlock meaningful ownership, not simply a larger logo.




PAGE 14 — IN-KIND PARTNERSHIPS
In-kind partnerships are reserved for products and services that materially improve the
experience or reduce a significant production cost.

That may include food and beverage, furniture, decor, florals, lighting, sound, production,
transportation, security, guest amenities, wellness products, recovery equipment, media, PR,
photography, video, marketing materials, signage, gifting, staffing or other event support.
Benefits are based on the fair value of the contribution, the importance of the product or service
to the event and how directly it touches the guest experience.

Depending on contribution level, in-kind partners may receive website and registration
recognition, onsite acknowledgment, product placement, sampling, VIP gifting, in-room
placement, social recognition, guest tickets, event access, select VIP parking, professional
photography where applicable and post-event recognition.




PAGE 15 — HYBRID PARTNERSHIPS
Some of the most valuable partnerships will combine financial support with product, equipment
or services.

A brand may contribute cash and product, cash and equipment, media and activation support,
hospitality and financial support, or another combination that makes sense for both sides.

Hybrid partnerships allow us to value the complete contribution and build a larger, more
strategic presence around the brand.

The structure is flexible. The value exchange is clear.




PAGE 16 — PARTNERSHIP LEVELS
Presenting Partner begins at $25,000 and offers the deepest integration across Down the
Rabbit Hole, including premium positioning, custom activation opportunities, high-level visibility,
category leadership and VIP access.

Signature Partner begins at $10,000 and is designed for brands that want to own or power a
major environment, experience or category.

Experience Partner begins at $5,000 and provides meaningful integration into a specific
activation, space or guest touchpoint.

Supporting Partner begins at $2,500 and offers focused visibility and selected integration.
In-kind partnerships are valued according to the contribution provided, while hybrid partnerships
are custom structured around total monetary, product and service value.




PAGE 17 — The Partnership value
A sponsorship should create business value beyond event recognition.

Depending on the partnership, that value may come through brand awareness, product trial,
product sampling, direct guest interaction, category ownership, VIP access, premium
placement, professional content, social visibility, press, relationship building and
post-event assets.

For experiential partners, the event can also create measurable engagement through
product distribution, QR scans, offer redemption, activation participation, leads or
opt-ins where appropriate, content captured and post-event digital activity.

The goal is to give partners a clear answer to one question:

What did this partnership create for us?




PAGE 18 — THE VALUE CONTINUES
AFTER THE EVENT
A strong partnership should continue working after the event ends.

Before the event, brands may receive digital placement, registration visibility, partner
announcements, social recognition and press opportunities.

During the event, value comes through guest interaction, activation ownership, product use,
hospitality, VIP placement, photography and video.

After the event, select partners can receive professional content, social recognition, press
continuation, approved photo and video assets and a partnership recap documenting agreed
deliverables and available engagement results.
The investment should create value across the full life of the event, not only during the hours
guests are on property.




PAGE 19 — SELECTED WITH
INTENTION
We are not filling every category or placing a sponsor on every surface.

Down the Rabbit Hole will work best when the brands inside it make the experience better.

That allows us to protect the visual environment, create meaningful category exclusivity and
give each partner enough space to matter.

The goal is not more sponsors.

It is stronger partnerships.
