THE COST

Dropped balls cost more than time.

A missed follow-up costs the deal. A forgotten deadline costs trust. Open loops cost sleep. Orí closes them with five parts that work as one system; the screen follows you as you scroll.

Worlds

Up to ten ongoing domains of life and business, each with its own planet, color and status. Worlds are never finished; they are tended.

Rocks and MVPs

Three active quarterly outcomes per World, with a definition of done and on- or off-track status. Star three across everything as your Most Valuable Priorities.

Projects, To-Dos and the Timeline

Finishable work sits inside a World. To-Dos show Needs Review at seven days and Stale at fourteen. Due dates, event starts and reminders stay distinct on one calendar.

Knowledge Base

Upload files, save notes and links, then ask a question scoped to a World or Project. Answers cite their sources or say plainly when the records cannot answer.

Output: the power section

Ask “What should I do today?” and Orí drafts the move from your own Rocks, notes and To-Dos: briefs, plans, weekly reviews. Eight original focus tracks fade the moment you hold to speak.
