WHAT IT DOES

One place for everything on your mind.

Most tools ask you to organize before you can think. CNFDNT OS lets you think first, then keeps the structure for you.

Voice-first intake

Hold the pill, say what is on your mind, release. The capture waits for your review, so nothing gets filed where you did not intend.

Worlds

Up to ten ongoing domains of life and business, each with its own planet, color and status. Worlds are never finished; they are tended.

Rocks and MVPs

Three active quarterly outcomes per World, with a definition of done and on- or off-track status. Star three across everything as your Most Valuable Priorities.

Projects and To-Dos that age

Finishable work sits inside a World. To-Dos show Needs Review at seven days and Stale at fourteen, so open loops surface instead of hiding.

Knowledge Base

Upload files, save notes and links, then ask a question scoped to a World or Project. Answers cite their sources or say plainly when the records cannot answer.

Output

Turn selected Rocks, notes and To-Dos into structured briefs, plans and drafts. Generated work never rewrites your records.

Timeline and reminders

Due dates, event starts and reminder times stay distinct. Choose "on due date", "one day before" or a custom time, and see everything on one calendar.

Focus music

Eight original tracks with a real signal meter. Music fades out the moment you hold to speak and returns when you are done.

YOUR UNIVERSE

See your whole life in orbit.

Every World gets a textured planet and a status you can read at a glance: on track, needs review, off track. Beneath the cards, a compact view of the Rocks and Projects with dates coming up.

  • Five World cards across wide screens, stacked on the phone
  • Notes with dates inside every World
  • Manual order that survives sorting and refreshes

ROCKS

Three outcomes a quarter. Three that matter most.

Rocks are the substantial outcomes for a planning period. Each World allows three active; the rest wait in a queue and promote automatically when a slot opens. Star up to three overall as MVPs and the Rocks tab keeps them on top.

  • Definition of done and due date on every Rock
  • On track or off track, reviewed weekly
  • Rock detail pages with notes, Projects and checkpoints

KNOWLEDGE

Ask your own records a question.

Upload PDFs, Word documents and text. Save notes and links. Then ask, scoped to all Worlds, one World or one Project. Every answer shows the note or file it drew from, and originals stay downloadable.

  • Twenty megabytes per file, originals preserved
  • Scoped search across notes, resources and uploads
  • Honest setup state until the model is configured

HOW IT WORKS

Speak. Sort. Move.

  1. 01

    Speak

    Open the app and hold the pill. Say the idea, the task, the worry. Or write it. The intake is the front door every time.

  2. 02

    Sort

    Review the capture and place it: a World, a Rock, a Project, a To-Do, an Issue or a note in Knowledge. Nothing is filed without you.

  3. 03

    Move

    Open the Worlds home. Your MVPs, upcoming dates and open loops are already in perspective. Ask "What should I do today?" and get a draft plan from your own records.
